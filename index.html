Why it's different

Most gym apps treat the Watch as a second screen. We didn't.

Three things we refuse to compromise on — one for how you train, one for who owns your data, one for how you learn.

True two-way

Wrist and pocket, in lockstep

Start, log, rest-time, cancel, or swipe pages from either device — whichever you touch first wins, and the other updates instantly. No internet, no lag.

Yours, locally

No account. No cloud lock-in.

Everything lives on your device. No sign-up, no subscription wall to open the app. Back up to a single file whenever you want, and take it with you.

Built to teach

See the muscle, learn the form

A live heat-map silhouette shows what each set works, with step-by-step how-to guides for all 124 movements — in your language.

Everything in the box

A serious tracker, not a stripped-down companion.

01

Right unit for every move

Weight × reps for lifting, time for planks, time + distance for the treadmill — each exercise tracked the way it's actually done, with a built-in stopwatch.

02

Apple Health, done properly

Live heart rate and real calories from the Watch during your session, written back to Health and your activity rings when you finish.

03

Synced rest timer

Countdown on both screens the moment you check a set, with a haptic buzz on your wrist at zero — even on a locked screen.

04

Previous, PRs & history

Last time's numbers sit right on the set card, personal records get a badge, and every workout is saved with a full history and progress charts.

05

Routines that fill themselves

Build a routine once; each workout starts pre-filled with the weights you actually lifted last time. kg or lbs, per set.

06

Share your session

Turn a finished workout into a clean card — stats, muscle silhouette, or minimal — and share it in a tap.
