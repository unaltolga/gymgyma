# GymGymA

Website for **GymGymA** — a gym workout tracker for iPhone and Apple Watch.

- **[Website](https://unaltolga.github.io/gymgyma/)**
- **[Privacy Policy](https://unaltolga.github.io/gymgyma/privacy-policy.html)**

## About the app

GymGymA runs on iPhone and Apple Watch at the same time, synced instantly and
without an internet connection. Build routines, log sets, reps and weight, run
rest timers, and track history and personal records. The Watch adds live heart
rate and calories, and finished workouts are saved to Apple Health.

Your data stays on your device: no account, no sign-in, no tracking, no
analytics. See the privacy policy for details.
